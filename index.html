Sign guide & settings

AI engine

Getting signs to register

  • Keep your face and both hands in frame, upper body visible.
  • Still signs: hold until the bar under the camera fills (about half a second).
  • Moving signs: do the movement once, clearly, at a normal speed.
  • Relax your hands between signs. Hands resting in your lap are ignored on purpose.
  • Two-handed signs: keep both hands in view the whole time.
  • Hand not recognised well? Use Calibrate to my hand on the Interpret tab.
A demo vocabulary of 100 common ISL-style words, each mapped to a handshape + place on your face/body + movement that a webcam can tell apart. It is not frame-matched to the official ISLRTC dictionary. Family rule: forehead = male relative, chin = female relative.
✨ Secret: point one finger and trace a full circle in the air, away from your face.

How SignBridge works

Architecture

1 · CameraRuns on your device. Video is never uploaded.
→
2 · TrackingMediaPipe: 21 points per hand (2 hands) + face keypoints.
→
3 · RecognitionJoint angles → finger states → handshape; motion; position relative to the face; per-user calibration; top-3 candidates.
4 · WordsHold-to-confirm, phrases, release-before-repeat, resting-hand filter.
→
5 · SentenceOffline rules instantly; Claude refines with candidates + conversation memory, tone, language.
→
6 · SpeechBrowser text-to-speech; speech-to-text for the other person.

Solid (tested)

  • Finger and handshape analysis is rotation-independent; tested on simulated hands with noise.
  • All 100 signs are distinct and recognise themselves in simulation.
  • Sentence engine, phrases, tone, AI request/response handling and fallbacks have automated tests.
  • Camera errors, blocked models, offline AI: each shows a clear message and the page keeps working.

Honest limits

  • The 100 signs are a designed demo vocabulary, not the official ISLRTC dictionary.
  • Accuracy on real hands and real lighting is not yet measured with real signers. That is the plan for the next round.
  • Single signs and short phrases only; no continuous sentence-level sign recognition.
  • Hindi/Telugu/Tamil translation needs the AI; spoken output depends on the voices installed in the browser.
